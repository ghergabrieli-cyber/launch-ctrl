FAQ

BEFORE YOU TURN THE KEY

The questions worth asking before a build starts.

How long does a website take?

It depends on scope. A focused build can move quickly; multilingual, e-commerce or integration-heavy projects need more time. The final timeline is agreed after scope review.

Do I own the website?

Ownership and handoff are defined in the final proposal. The goal is a clear, documented setup — not locking the client into mystery infrastructure.

Can you handle hosting and domains?

We can set up or guide hosting, deployment and domain configuration appropriate to the project. Third-party hosting and domain fees remain separate unless explicitly included.

Can you write or rewrite the copy?

Yes. Copywriting, restructuring and localization can be part of the build. Final language scope is agreed before work starts.

Which languages can you build in?

English, Romanian and French can be handled directly. German, Italian and Russian can be supported through multilingual architecture and localization with final human/native QA before publication.

How do revisions work?

Revision rounds and decision points are defined in the proposal so feedback stays useful and the project does not drift endlessly.

Can you build e-commerce, booking or integrations?

Yes. Those are scoped separately because complexity can vary dramatically depending on payments, catalogue size, providers, APIs and operational requirements.

What happens after launch?

You can keep the site independently or use PIT CREW for ongoing changes, additions, monitoring and support.
