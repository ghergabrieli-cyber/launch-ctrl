BUILD 005 / SELECTED STUDIO BUILD

REASON AXIS

A cognitive and hiring-assessment platform with two clear entry paths: train core skills or prepare for real hiring assessments.

IN DEVELOPMENT / STUDIO BUILD
01 / CHALLENGE

THE BUSINESS FIRST.

Assessment products can feel either clinical and intimidating or like generic quiz apps. The product needs credibility, measurable progress and strong task clarity without losing energy.

02 / DIRECTION

NO TEMPLATE LOGIC.

Midnight navy, cool white and restrained electric accents with abstract axes, coordinates and progress systems rather than literal space imagery.

03 / SYSTEM

WHAT THE BUILD NEEDS TO DO.

Training modules, hiring-assessment preparation, performance measurement, progress states and future skill worlds that can scale beyond one test type.

04 / LANGUAGES

MARKET READY.

International-first architecture, with English as the primary product language and room for localized markets as the platform grows.
