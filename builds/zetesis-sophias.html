BUILD 006 / SELECTED STUDIO BUILD

ZETESIS SOPHIAS

A professional-learning umbrella designed to take a learner from zero knowledge to real working judgment through structured worlds, missions and applied scenarios.

IN DEVELOPMENT / STUDIO BUILD
01 / CHALLENGE

THE BUSINESS FIRST.

Professional education often assumes too much prior knowledge or turns into walls of theory. This build must make complex work systems understandable, sequential and genuinely usable.

02 / DIRECTION

NO TEMPLATE LOGIC.

Mission-based progression, professional worlds, characters and structured learning states — serious enough for career skills, engaging enough to keep momentum.

03 / SYSTEM

WHAT THE BUILD NEEDS TO DO.

World architecture, missions, scenario learning, procurement/supply-chain foundations, future SAP labs and expansion into other professions.

04 / LANGUAGES

MARKET READY.

The professional platform is designed for international use, with language-specific experiences separated from the core professional-learning structure.
