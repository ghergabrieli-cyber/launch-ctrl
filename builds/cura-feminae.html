BUILD 008 / SELECTED STUDIO BUILD

CURA FEMINAE

A private gynecology practice concept centered on one physician, clear patient information and a calm contemporary identity.

IN DEVELOPMENT / STUDIO BUILD
01 / CHALLENGE

THE BUSINESS FIRST.

Gynecology websites can easily become generic, overly clinical or visually infantilizing. The site needs seriousness, privacy and warmth without losing medical clarity.

02 / DIRECTION

NO TEMPLATE LOGIC.

Dusty light lilac, deep plum, warm ivory and muted mauve with a clean healthcare structure and dedicated pages instead of one long information stream.

03 / SYSTEM

WHAT THE BUILD NEEDS TO DO.

Doctor/about information, services, patient guidance, contact and future appointment functionality with a structure that can expand if additional specialists join later.

04 / LANGUAGES

MARKET READY.

Romanian as the primary patient language with English architecture available for international patients and future expansion.
