BUILD 004 / PORTFOLIO BUILD

CODEX NOCTURNUM

An archive-and-shop concept for witchcraft, folklore and symbolic material that separates history, folklore, modern practice and interpretation instead of flattening them together.

CURRENT STATE / ACTIVE BUILD
01 / CHALLENGE

THE BUSINESS FIRST.

The experience needs atmosphere without sacrificing clarity, provenance or usability. It must feel like entering an archive, not a Halloween storefront.

02 / DIRECTION

NO TEMPLATE LOGIC.

Dark archival typography, controlled ritual imagery, cinematic entry moments and a clear hierarchy between evidence, folklore and modern symbolic material.

03 / SYSTEM

WHAT THE BUILD NEEDS TO DO.

Archive taxonomy, multilingual shop architecture, candle and botanical product systems, session-based intro behaviour and a structure that can support deep editorial content alongside commerce.

04 / LANGUAGES

MARKET READY.

Romanian, English, French, German and Italian are planned for the broader shop/archive architecture, with final commercial copy reviewed before release.
