BUILD 003 / PORTFOLIO BUILD

LA VATRA

A cultural commerce brand turning Romanian and European memory, craft references and emotional storytelling into a modern digital shop.

CURRENT STATE / ACTIVE BUILD
01 / CHALLENGE

THE BUSINESS FIRST.

Heritage can become cliché very quickly. The build has to feel rooted and recognisable without looking like a souvenir store or a generic rustic template.

02 / DIRECTION

NO TEMPLATE LOGIC.

Embroidery, carved wood, paper texture and regional references used as a visual language rather than decoration piled onto every screen.

03 / SYSTEM

WHAT THE BUILD NEEDS TO DO.

Multicurrency commerce, Romanian and English content, product families, editorial storytelling and room for planners, archive-style products and future collections.

04 / LANGUAGES

MARKET READY.

Romanian and English are core; the commerce structure is prepared for additional market-specific localization as the catalogue expands.
