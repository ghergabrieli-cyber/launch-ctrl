PRIVACY

When you submit a project brief, LAUNCH CTRL receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, business, email address, website, project scope and notes. This information is used only to review your request, respond to you and prepare a potential proposal.

Project briefs are not sold. They may be processed by infrastructure and email-delivery providers only as needed to operate the website and deliver your request. Retention should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for project communication, legal obligations and business records.

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information by replying to the project correspondence you receive from LAUNCH CTRL. A fuller controller identity, legal basis, retention period and contact details will be added before the final public commercial launch.
